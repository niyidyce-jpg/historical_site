National war museum (Abia state)

The National War Museum is a major historical museum located in Umuahia, Abia State, Nigeria. It is the only war museum in Nigeria and was established in 1985 to preserve and showcase the country's military history, especially the Nigerian Civil War (1967–1970).


Olumo rock (ogun state)

The Olumo Rock is one of Nigeria's most famous tourist attractions. It is located in Abeokuta, Ogun State, and stands about 137 metres above sea level. The rock served as a natural fortress and refuge for the Egba people during the inter-tribal wars of the 19th century.


Emir of Kano's place (kano state)

The Emir of Kano’s Palace, also known as Gidan Rumfa, is one of the oldest and most important traditional palaces in Nigeria. It is located in Kano City, Kano State, and serves as the residence of the Emir of Kano. The palace was built by King Muhammadu Rumfa during his reign from 1463 to 1499 and has remained a symbol of Kano's rich cultural heritage for over 500 years.


Kano City Walls (Kano State)

The Kano City Walls are one of the most important historical landmarks in Nigeria. They are located in Kano State and surround the ancient city of Kano. The walls were originally built between the 11th and 14th centuries to protect the city from invasions and to strengthen the security of the powerful Kano Kingdom.


Point of No Return (Lagos State)

The Point of No Return is a historic site located in Badagry, Nigeria. It is one of the most significant landmarks associated with the transatlantic slave trade. During the slave trade era, enslaved Africans were taken through this route to waiting ships that transported them across the Atlantic Ocean to the Americas.


Zuma Rock (FCT, Abuja)

The Zuma Rock is one of Nigeria's most famous natural landmarks. It is located along the Abuja–Kaduna Expressway near Abuja and stands at about 725 meters (2,379 feet) above its surroundings. Because of its massive size and unique appearance, it is often called the "Gateway to Abuja."


First Storey Building in Nigeria (Lagos State)

The First Storey Building in Nigeria is a famous historical monument located in Badagry. It is widely recognized as Nigeria's first storey building and one of the country's most important historical landmarks. The foundation of the building was laid in 1842 by missionaries of the Church Missionary Society (CMS), and it was completed in 1845.


The Oranmiyan Staff (Osun State)

The Oranmiyan Staff, also known as Opa Oranmiyan, is a famous historical monument located in Ile-Ife, Osun State. It is a tall granite monolith that stands about 5.5 metres (18–20 feet) high and is one of the most important cultural landmarks of the Yoruba people.


Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove (Osun State)

The Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove is one of Nigeria's most important cultural and spiritual heritage sites. Located on the outskirts of Osogbo in Osun State, the grove is a sacred forest dedicated to Osun, the Yoruba goddess of fertility, love, and prosperity.
